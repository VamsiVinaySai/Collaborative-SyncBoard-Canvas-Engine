⚡
Real-Time Sync
Every brushstroke broadcasts instantly over a persistent WebSocket connection. What you draw, every teammate sees in under 50ms — no refresh, no delay, no polling.
🔌 Socket.IO · WebSocket
<50
↩️
Smart Undo / Redo
Per-user undo tracked server-side. Undo only your own strokes — never a teammate's. Redo stack preserved until you draw again.
🖱️
Live Cursors
Every connected user's cursor appears in real-time, color-coded and labelled with their name. Throttled to 30fps.
🛠️
Drawing Toolkit
Brush, eraser, line, rectangle, circle. Adjustable size from 1–50px. 12 preset colors plus full custom picker.
🔗
Zero Friction
No accounts. No downloads. Share a 12-character Room ID and anyone can join. Rooms auto-delete when the last user leaves.
📥
Export & Download
Download the full canvas as a merged PNG — both your layer and your teammates' layers composited into one clean file.
Built with Socket.IO + HTML5 Canvas
